# Calendars

**Location:** Platform → Platform Tools → Calendars

Calendars manages market and business-day reference data used by assets, portfolios, and scheduled
workflows.

## What the list shows

The list shows calendar name, unique identifier, type, timezone, and source. Search is available.

## Detail view

- **Overview** shows the complete calendar identity.
- **Summary** presents canonical coverage and status.
- **Dates** lists business days and holidays.
- **Sessions** lists open and close times with their timezone.
- **Events** lists dated market or operational events.

## Available actions

- **Create calendar** creates the identity and validity range.
- **Edit calendar** changes mutable display, timezone, or metadata fields.
- **Add date**, **Add session**, and **Add event** append the corresponding dated record.
- **Delete calendar** removes the calendar when backend constraints allow it.

Use the calendar's timezone consistently. Before adding a session or event, confirm that its date is
inside the calendar's intended validity range.
